Keep it on track

Three things that protect your 30 days.

The guarantee is about speed from sign-off, so a little preparation on your side keeps every day on the clock.

  • Settle your design before sign-off: design changes are outside the guarantee.
  • Have your site ready: site readiness is outside the guarantee.
  • Plan for +10 days if you are in North East India or choose a finish that is out of inventory.
