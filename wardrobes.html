Wardrobes at Homworks

Homworks builds L-shaped corner wardrobes and walk-in wardrobes in its own factory, designed around your room dimensions and storage needs.

Boards are E0 grade, and furniture is made to the millimetre so fitting is smooth.

Wardrobe styles

Two ways to store more.

Wardrobe

L-shaped wardrobe

For those who want to use every bedroom corner – often fitted with a sliding-door system, in a corner with good light.

Wardrobe

Walk-in wardrobe

The dream wardrobe and an epitome of luxury – best built inside an unused room, and lit like a showroom.

Finishes

Choose the finish you will live with.

Wardrobes come in lamination, membrane, acrylic and PU paint finishes, in a wide range of designs, colours and finishing options.

  • Lamination: the most common finish; withstands rough surfaces.
  • Acrylic: a glassy, almost mirror-like surface with a plush sheen.
  • PU paint: protects the cabinets and lasts longer.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.
