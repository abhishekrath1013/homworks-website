Questions

Before you call

Book a free consultation. A Homworks designer calls you within 24 hours to understand your home, and you can consult by video call or visit an experience centre.

Homworks aims to finish your interiors in just 30 working days from design sign-off, or it’s free*. The clock starts when you approve your 3D design and fixed quote. The guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes and site readiness, and adds 10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.

The cost depends on your home size, finish and the rooms you choose. After your free consultation, your designer confirms a fixed quote, and Homworks prices module by module so the quotation is as close as possible to the final cost.

Homworks has centres in Coimbatore, Chennai, Bangalore, Erode, Hyderabad and Guwahati.
