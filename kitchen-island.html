Galley kitchen

One-Wall kitchen

Every function lines up along a single wall — the layout of choice for studios and homes that want the kitchen to open into the living room.

Peninsular kitchen

An L-shaped layout extended with a connected peninsula — extra counter and seating without the floor space a full island needs.

Questions

Island kitchen questions

Homworks recommends the Island kitchen for Villas & 4BHK+, typically in a footprint of 150+ sqft. A freestanding counter sits at the heart of the room — for larger homes that want the kitchen to be where everyone ends up.

A second prep zone. The island can be a chopping station, a breakfast bar, or both. The social centre of the home. Turns cooking into something everyone gathers around, not a room apart. Flexible by design. Add a hob, a sink or just seating to the island face — your call. Built for larger homes. Needs more floor area than other layouts, so it suits villas and bigger 4BHKs best.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.

Homworks aims to finish your interiors in just 30 working days from design sign-off, or it’s free*. The clock starts when you approve your 3D design and fixed quote. The guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes and site readiness, and adds 10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.
