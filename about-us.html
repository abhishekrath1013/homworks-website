Who is Homworks?

Homworks is a home interiors company that designs, manufactures and installs furniture and interiors for the way India lives now.

Interiors are engineered in Homworks’ own Coimbatore factory and fitted at your home by the Homworks team. Homworks operates as Stylcove Modulars Pvt Ltd, with centres in six cities.

What we believe

01

Every project starts with a conversation about how you live.

03

Built to last

A 10-year warranty and an after-sales service to stand behind the work.

The factory

Made in our own Coimbatore factory.

Our own manufacturing facility runs CNC and flat-lamination machinery imported from Italy and Germany. Each material is tested twice: when it reaches the factory and when it is issued to a project.

  • An in-house material testing lab
  • Own installation team
  • Site carpentry kept for fillers, end panels and visible panels
