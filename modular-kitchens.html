Modular kitchens at Homworks

Homworks makes modular kitchens in six layouts – L-shaped, U-shaped, galley, one-wall, peninsular and island – and six styles, designed around your floor plan and made in its own factory.

Kitchen carcasses use ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 boards, and every project carries a 10-year warranty.

Built for a working kitchen

Heat, steam and years of daily use.

Kitchens are damp, hot and wet places. Homworks tests, bonds and spaces every kitchen with that in mind.

  • 72-hour boiling test – every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water, with results recorded before use.
  • PUR hot-melt edge-banding – ideal for high-temperature environments, withstanding up to 80° C.
  • 18mm wall gap – keeps the core materials away from wall dampness, so the product lasts longer.
  • Double carcass – a single unit can be replaced without disturbing the other cabinets.

Yes. Every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours, edges are banded with PUR hot-melt glue that withstands up to 80° C, and an 18mm gap is kept between the wall and the carcass so the core stays away from wall dampness.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.
