L-Shaped kitchen questions

Homworks recommends the L-Shaped kitchen for 2–3BHK homes, typically in a footprint of 70–110 sqft. Two adjoining walls meet at a corner to free up the centre of the room — the most requested layout for Indian homes because it works in almost any footprint.

Shorter walk, less waiting. A tight work triangle between hob, sink and fridge means less walking for the same amount of cooking. The centre stays open. No cabinetry runs through the middle of the room — ideal if you want a breakfast table or open floor. Scales with your home. Works as a compact corner kitchen or a full family kitchen, in the same basic shape. Easiest to ventilate. A single uninterrupted wall makes it simple to plan a window and chimney together.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.
