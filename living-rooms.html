Living rooms

A living room that feels like home.

Your living room is where conversations happen, families come together and everyday moments become memories.

At Homworks, we create living spaces that balance comfort, functionality and design. From customised TV units and elegant wall panels to console tables and living partitions, every element is planned to complement your space and lifestyle.

TV units

Choose the TV unit that fits your style.

Whether you prefer a unit that sits on the floor or one that floats on the wall, we have designs to suit your living room.

  • Floor-standing TV unit – a low cabinet that sits on the floor, giving you plenty of storage and a complete look around your TV.
  • Wall-hung TV unit – a floating TV unit that keeps the floor clear and gives your living room a neat, spacious look. A bottom support can also be added if needed.

Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Choose from lamination, membrane, acrylic and PU paint finishes in different colours and designs.

Your furniture is made at our Coimbatore facility using advanced CNC and flat-lamination machinery, then delivered and fitted at your home. Final adjustments and finishing are done at the site wherever needed.

We aim to complete your interiors within 30 working days from design sign-off, as per our 30-day promise. Certain conditions and exclusions apply.
