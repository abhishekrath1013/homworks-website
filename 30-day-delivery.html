Delivery questions

Homworks aims to finish your interiors in just 30 working days from design sign-off, or it’s free*. The clock starts when you approve your 3D design and fixed quote. The guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes and site readiness, and adds 10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.

Minor changes are usually possible before production begins. Larger changes once production has started can affect your timeline and cost, and the 30-day guarantee excludes design changes.

Yes. Furniture is made in the Homworks factory in Coimbatore, using CNC and flat-lamination machinery imported from Italy and Germany, and then fitted at your home. Site carpentry is kept for fillers, end panels and visible panels.

Every Homworks project carries a 10-year warranty on cabinetry, carcass and mechanisms. Ask your designer for the written terms before you sign off.
