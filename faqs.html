The basics

The cost depends on your home size, finish and the rooms you choose. After your free consultation, your designer confirms a fixed quote, and Homworks prices module by module so the quotation is as close as possible to the final cost.

Homworks aims to finish your interiors in just 30 working days from design sign-off, or it’s free*. The clock starts when you approve your 3D design and fixed quote. The guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes and site readiness, and adds 10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.

Book a free consultation. A Homworks designer calls you within 24 hours to understand your home, and you can consult by video call or visit an experience centre.

Homworks has centres in Coimbatore, Chennai, Bangalore, Erode, Hyderabad and Guwahati.

Factory and installation

Your furniture parts are made in our factory and brought to your home for fitting. Cutting and fabrication happen off-site rather than turning your home into a carpentry workshop.

Factory fabrication reduces the cutting, sanding, dust, and noise at your property. Your home becomes the installation site, not the workshop. Some drilling and final adjustments may still be needed during fitting.

The factory-made parts are delivered to your property for assembly, positioning, leveling, and securing. The focus is on fitting the furniture into your space rather than building it from raw materials on-site.

Factory-made does not have to mean standard-sized. Furniture can be designed around your room dimensions, storage needs, and chosen finishes, with the design and measurements confirmed before production.

Most fabrication happens in the factory. Installation may still involve drilling, fixing hardware, and small fitting adjustments. This reduces on-site carpentry, but does not mean installation will be completely dust- or noise-free.

Yes. Furniture is made in the Homworks factory in Coimbatore, using CNC and flat-lamination machinery imported from Italy and Germany, and then fitted at your home. Site carpentry is kept for fillers, end panels and visible panels.

Materials and finishes

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.

Yes. Every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours, edges are banded with PUR hot-melt glue that withstands up to 80° C, and an 18mm gap is kept between the wall and the carcass so the core stays away from wall dampness.

Homworks offers lamination, membrane, acrylic and PU paint finishes, in a wide range of designs, colours and finishing options for every room.

Changes and warranty

Minor changes are usually possible before production begins. Larger changes once production has started can affect your timeline and cost, and the 30-day guarantee excludes design changes.

Every Homworks project carries a 10-year warranty on cabinetry, carcass and mechanisms. Ask your designer for the written terms before you sign off.
