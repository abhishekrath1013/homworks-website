Lamination

The most common finish, and it withstands any rough surface. It brings out a natural, seamless look. Its understated appearance lets the material take centre stage, creating a cohesive finish that complements a range of interior styles—from clean, contemporary spaces to warm, traditional settings.

Membrane

A unique finish with a natural appeal, at home in traditional and contemporary designs — in matte, gloss, wooden and many colours.

Acrylic

A glossy, almost mirror-like surface with a plush sheen — non-toxic, and available in a wide range of colours.

PU paint

A finish that elevates the space, protects the cabinets and lasts longer — sturdier and stronger.

      How we build and install your space

      Made in our factory. Fitted in your home. Learn how our approach keeps furniture fabrication off-site and focuses on installation at your place.

      Your furniture parts are made in our factory and brought to your home for fitting. Cutting and fabrication happen off-site rather than turning your home into a carpentry workshop.

      Factory fabrication reduces the cutting, sanding, dust, and noise at your property. Your home becomes the installation site, not the workshop. Some drilling and final adjustments may still be needed during fitting.

      The factory-made parts are delivered to your property for assembly, positioning, leveling, and securing. The focus is on fitting the furniture into your space rather than building it from raw materials on-site.

      Factory-made does not have to mean standard-sized. Furniture can be designed around your room dimensions, storage needs, and chosen finishes, with the design and measurements confirmed before production.

      Most fabrication happens in the factory. Installation may still involve drilling, fixing hardware, and small fitting adjustments. This reduces on-site carpentry, but does not mean installation will be completely dust- or noise-free.
