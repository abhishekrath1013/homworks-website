How does Homworks work?

Homworks works in three stages – Get, Set and Go – covering 15 steps from meeting your designer to installation, quality check and handover.

Consult by video call or visit an experience centre, share your ideas and preferences, approve your 3D design, and your interiors are made in our factory and installed by our team.

In detail

What happens, and when.

  1. 01 · GET

    Meet your designer

    Consult by video call or visit an experience centre. Share your ideas, needs, and design preferences.

  2. 02 · GET

    Initial design and booking

    Explore styles and get answers from design experts. Then book with an advance and your designer measures the site.

  3. 03 · SET

    3D model and design freeze

    Approve your 3D design and fixed quote. That is Day 0 of your 30 days.*

  4. 04 · SET

    BOM and production drawings

    Production drawings and bill of materials are prepared, and the site is validated before production begins.

  5. 05 · GO

    Factory production

    Your modules move into our own factory – not a third-party workshop.

  6. 06 · GO

    Dispatch, installation and handover

    Our own installation team fits your home, with a 100+ point quality check before final handover. Your 10-year warranty begins at handover.

* Guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes, site readiness. +10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.
