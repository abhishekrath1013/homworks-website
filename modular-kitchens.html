Six kitchen layouts

6 layouts. Find the one that fits your home.

Whether you have a compact kitchen or a spacious one, we have a layout that works for your space.

01
Best for 2–3 BHK Homes

L-Shaped Kitchen

Two adjoining counters make good use of corner spaces, leaving more room to move around. A popular choice for Indian homes.

02
Best for Larger Kitchens

U-Shaped Kitchen

Three connected counters give you more storage and workspace, making cooking easier for the whole family.

03
Best for Compact Apartments

Galley Kitchen

Two rows of counters face each other, keeping everything within easy reach. Perfect for narrow kitchens.

04
Best for Studios & 1BHK Homes

One-Wall Kitchen

Everything is arranged along one wall, making it a simple and space-saving choice for compact homes.

05
Best for Open-Plan 3BHK Homes

Peninsular Kitchen

An extended counter gives you extra workspace and a place to sit, without needing a separate kitchen island.

06
Best for Villas & 4BHK+ Homes

Island Kitchen

A separate counter in the middle gives you extra workspace and storage, while creating a space for family and friends to gather.

Built for everyday cooking

Made to handle heat, water and everyday use.

Your kitchen goes through a lot every day. That’s why we pay attention to the materials, finishes and small details that help it last longer.

01

72-Hour Boiling Test

Our BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours before being used in your kitchen.

02

PUR Hot-Melt Edge Banding

Strong edge bonding that can withstand temperatures up to 80°C, with edge strength above 7 Newtons.

03

18mm Gap from the Wall

A small gap between the wall and cabinet helps protect the core material from wall dampness.

04

Double Carcass

Each cabinet unit is designed for easier replacement, without disturbing the other cabinets.
