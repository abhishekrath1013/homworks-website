Modular kitchens at Homworks

Homworks makes modular kitchens in six layouts – L-shaped, U-shaped, galley, one-wall, peninsular and island – and six styles, designed around your floor plan and made in its own factory.

Kitchen carcasses use ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 boards, and every project carries a 10-year warranty.

Choose a layout

Six layouts. One right answer for your home.

Each layout suits a different home size and way of cooking.

01
Best for 2–3BHK homes

L-Shaped kitchen

Two adjoining walls meet at a corner to free up the centre of the room — the most requested layout for Indian homes because it works in almost any footprint.

02
Best for Large families

U-Shaped kitchen

Three connected walls of cabinetry give you the most storage and counter space of any layout — built for households that cook often, and together.

03
Best for Compact apartments

Galley kitchen

Worktops face each other across a single walkway — the fastest layout to cook in, and the smartest use of a narrow room.

04
Best for Studios & 1BHKs

One-Wall kitchen

Every function lines up along a single wall — the layout of choice for studios and homes that want the kitchen to open into the living room.

05
Best for Open-plan 3BHKs

Peninsular kitchen

An L-shaped layout extended with a connected peninsula — extra counter and seating without the floor space a full island needs.

06
Best for Villas & 4BHK+

Island kitchen

A freestanding counter sits at the heart of the room — for larger homes that want the kitchen to be where everyone ends up.

Built for a working kitchen

Heat, steam and years of daily use.

Kitchens are damp, hot and wet places. Homworks tests, bonds and spaces every kitchen with that in mind.

01

72-hour boiling test

Every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours, with results recorded before it is used in projects.

02

PUR hot-melt edge-banding

Ideal for high-temperature environments like kitchens, withstanding up to 80° C. Edge strength is above 7 Newtons.

03

18mm gap from the wall

The 18mm gap keeps the core materials away from wall dampness, so the product lasts longer.

04

Double carcass

A single unit can be replaced easily without disturbing the other cabinets.

Questions

Modular kitchen questions

Yes. Every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours, edges are banded with PUR hot-melt glue that withstands up to 80° C, and an 18mm gap is kept between the wall and the carcass so the core stays away from wall dampness.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.

Homworks aims to finish your interiors in just 30 working days from design sign-off, or it’s free*. The clock starts when you approve your 3D design and fixed quote. The guarantee excludes client-side delays, design changes and site readiness, and adds 10 days for North East India and out-of-inventory finishes.

The cost depends on your home size, finish and the rooms you choose. After your free consultation, your designer confirms a fixed quote, and Homworks prices module by module so the quotation is as close as possible to the final cost.
