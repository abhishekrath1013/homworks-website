Warranty questions

Every Homworks project carries a 10-year warranty on cabinetry, carcass and mechanisms. Ask your designer for the written terms before you sign off.

Homworks uses ISI-certified BWP / HDHMR E0 double-side post-laminated boards as the core material in every carcass. E0-grade boards carry no formaldehyde.

Yes. Every BWP-grade plywood is tested in boiling water for 72 hours, edges are banded with PUR hot-melt glue that withstands up to 80° C, and an 18mm gap is kept between the wall and the carcass so the core stays away from wall dampness.
